# NO ONE LASTS FOREVER

### *Arte, mortalidad y la marca que dejamos.*

</div>

---

<div align="center">

Inspirado en un fragmento particular de este tema, en el que **David Cronenberg** recita un *spoken word* sobre el arte, nuestra propia mortalidad y el legado que dejamos atrás.

Puede que nadie permanezca para siempre, pero cada persona deja una marca.  
Una pequeña huella en la historia.

</div>

<br>

As I was lying there on the sidewalk,
I did not think about art at all.

I— I thought about people.
I thought about life.

“You feel that people feel they could
do better in life?”

Yeah. Yes, sure, sure.

But— and that's why I like being
close to my house.

Somehow that was comforting,
even though it makes no sense,
you know?

But, um, no,
I wasn't thinking about art.

And I wasn't thinking about art
as endurance, as— as, uh,
immortality, you know?

I mean, we can talk about immortal
artists of various kinds.

And it— it can turn out that, yes,
some artists have created art that
has really survived thousands of years.

And that's fantastic.

But experientially, for them:

They are dead.
They are gone.
They are not experiencing that.

These are true things,
and thank goodness for them.

I— I— I really feel, um,
and you hear me assuring you that:

Oblivion is freedom.
